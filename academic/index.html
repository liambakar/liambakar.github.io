About

I'm a PhD student at the University of Cambridge working at the intersection of human-centered AI and healthcare. My research spans language models, multimodal sensing, and personalized health technologies, with a focus on making AI both effective and healthy to interact with.

Research interests

Human-centered AI
Designing intelligent systems that prioritize human agency and well-being.
AI for health
Turning patient-generated, physiological, and behavioral data into useful insights for people and clinicians.
Multimodal intelligent systems
Combining language models, sensing, and personalization to understand real-world health context.
